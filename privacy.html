Privacy Policy

Effective May 23, 2026

This page explains what information the Fantasy Football Auction Draft Simulator (the "site") collects, how it's used, and the third-party services involved. The site is a free hobby project, not a commercial product, and we try to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

The only personal information the site collects is what you choose to enter:

  • Team name — used purely to label your team in the simulated draft. Never sent anywhere.
  • Email address (optional) — you can enter an email on the setup screen so that, after a draft, the site can email you a summary of your final roster. If you leave this field blank, no email is collected.

Everything else about your draft (player picks, bids, budgets, results) is kept only in your browser's memory while the page is open. Closing the tab clears it. The site does not run user accounts, does not save profiles, and does not track activity across sessions.

How we use your email

If you provide an email address and click "Email Results" at the end of a draft, your email is sent to FormSubmit, a third-party form-forwarding service. FormSubmit then delivers a single message to your inbox containing your draft summary. We do not store your email, do not add it to any list, and do not send marketing.

FormSubmit's own privacy practices are described on their website. Their service is what physically delivers the email; the site itself has no server-side database of users.

Cookies and advertising

The site itself does not set any cookies and does not use analytics. However, the site displays advertisements served by third-party ad networks (currently Google AdSense or a successor network). These networks may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Show ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

Google's use of advertising cookies is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads or visit aboutads.info for opt-out tools across many networks.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Use the simulator without entering an email — no personal data is collected at all.
  • Clear your browser's cookies to remove any advertising cookies set during your visit.
  • Email us (below) with any questions or requests.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective" date above will be revised when we do. Material changes will be noted on this page.
