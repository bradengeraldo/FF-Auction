About this simulator

This is a practice tool for auction-style fantasy football drafts — the format where every team starts with the same budget and bids on each player, rather than picking in turn from a list. Auction drafts let you build any roster you can afford, which makes them more strategic but also harder to prepare for, since there's no draft board to memorize.

How to use it

Enter your team name and tweak the settings above, then click Start Draft. You'll be dropped into a live 12-team auction with a $200 budget and 16 roster spots. When it's your turn to nominate, search for the player you want to throw into the pool and set a starting bid. When the other 11 computer GMs nominate, you decide whether to bid, increase the bid, or let them have the player at the current price.

Strategy ideas to practice

  • Stars-and-scrubs — spend most of your budget on 2–3 elite players and fill the rest of your roster cheaply.
  • Balanced build — spread $15–$30 across most of your starters and avoid any single dominant player.
  • Price enforcer — bid up players you don't want to drain opponents' budgets, but be careful not to get stuck owning them.
  • Late-round savings — figure out the minimum you can spend on QB, K, and DST so you have more to work with on RB and WR.

What this site is not

This is a free hobby project. It is not affiliated with the NFL, ESPN, Yahoo, or any actual fantasy platform. Player values are baseline estimates and should not be your only source for real draft prep. Use it to get comfortable with bidding pace and budget management, then check current rankings before your real draft.
